Free-first and private by design.

This V1 uses an on-device Orish mission engine. It combines age, toy and world choices locally in the browser. No child name, photo, account or conversation is required, and this prototype does not send child data to an external AI provider.

That lets us test whether families love the play experience before paying for generative AI. A future AI upgrade can sit behind Orish with parent controls, moderation and strict child-safety rules.
