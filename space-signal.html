ORISH'S SIGNAL GUIDE

How do signal waves work?

A signal wave carries information. We can compare its shape instead of guessing what it means.

⌁

Amplitude

The height of the wave. A taller wave usually means a stronger signal.

∿∿∿

Frequency

How many waves pass in the same amount of time. More waves means a higher frequency.

▆ · ▆ · ▆

Interval

The space or time between pulses. Equal gaps can show a repeating pattern.

◉ →

Direction

Compare where the signal is strongest to work out where it came from.
