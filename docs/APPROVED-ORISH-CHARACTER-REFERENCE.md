# Approved Orish Character Reference — 23 August 2026

This is the locked visual direction for Orish across the premium build.

## Identity
- Orish is six years old in the core visual reference.
- Young Black boy with a rich medium-deep brown skin tone: darker than the first premium concepts, but not the very darkest variation tested.
- Taller, age-believable six-year-old proportions. Do not use the short/toddler-like proportions from earlier concepts.
- Neat plaits/braids, expressive warm face, large readable eyes, confident friendly smile.
- Premium stylised 3D animated-film quality.

## Core outfit
- Navy / black explorer jacket and hoodie.
- Bright cyan / electric blue accents.
- Restrained warm gold AT THE CODE details.
- Cargo-style trousers and trainers.
- Age-appropriate, modern, practical explorer styling.

## World styling
- Bright, warm, colourful and cinematic.
- Do not make the full product dark navy or overwhelmingly blue.
- Combine cyan/navy with sunlight, warm gold, lavender, violet, coral, peach, mint, green, soft pink highlights and cosmic purple.
- Floating adventure worlds, waterfalls, magical science/future architecture, nature, warm skies and visible depth.
- Premium and exciting while remaining child-safe and welcoming to girls and boys.

## Continuity rule
Every future Orish image — Core, Explorer, Science, Space, Detective, Kitchen, Maker, Calm and Mission — must look like the same child. Clothing and environment can change; facial identity, skin tone, plaits, age and proportions must remain consistent.

## Brand separation rule
- Orish’s World is an AT THE CODE children’s product.
- Never place Elegua’s Crossroads branding, wording, logos, watermarks or "Powered by Elegua’s Crossroads" on Orish’s World assets or screens.

## Rejected directions
- Blue O/body placeholder.
- Random unrelated children used instead of Orish.
- Locs/dreadlocks instead of the approved plaits.
- Toddler-short proportions.
- Skin tone substantially lighter than the approved reference.
- Skin tone substantially darker than the approved reference.
- Mostly-dark-blue screens with little warmth or colour variety.
- Flat clip-art or generic education-app imagery.
