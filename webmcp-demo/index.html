Live WebMCP tool showcase

Tool 1 — Get a learning activity

get_orish_learning_activity

Returns an age-appropriate activity by topic: rocket, reading or science.

Tap “Run demo” to see the structured response.

Tool 2 — Get the Too-Fast Rocket lesson

get_too_fast_rocket_lesson

Returns the story lesson plus a parent discussion prompt.

Tap “Run demo” to see the structured response.

Tool 3 — Plan a mini learning session

plan_orish_learning_session

Combines a topic and age into a short story-to-activity learning path.

Tap “Run demo” to see the structured response.

Why this matters

Orish’s World is not trying to replace parents or turn every moment into screen time. The aim is to let AI help families move from a digital story into a useful real-world learning activity, while the website defines the available actions and keeps grown-up guidance visible.
