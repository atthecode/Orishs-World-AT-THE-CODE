# Are We Alone? — V1.26 evidence investigation

This child-facing investigation treats extraterrestrial life as a genuine scientific question while separating confirmed facts, claims, unresolved observations and proof.

## Teaching rule

- Confirmed exoplanets are taught as established astronomy.
- The search for extraterrestrial life is taught as active science.
- Haim Eshed’s 2020 “Galactic Federation” statement is taught as a claim made by a credentialed person, not proof of contact.
- UAP means unidentified from the available data; unresolved does not mean extraterrestrial.
- The lesson explicitly models what would justify changing the conclusion: independently inspectable, repeatable, well-controlled evidence.
- If credible evidence changes, approved lesson content should change too.

## Child safety / privacy

No unrestricted web search is opened for children. Source organisation, title and lesson notes are presented in-app. No voice recordings, location, camera, uploads or private research notes are required.
