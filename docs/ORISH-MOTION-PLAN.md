# Orish Motion Plan

Approved direction: keep the exact approved Orish image/identity and add motion in stages.

## Stage 1 — immediate 2D motion
- Gentle breathing/idle float only; no identity or skin-tone change.
- Small parallax response to pointer/touch where appropriate.
- Reduced-motion support for accessibility.

## Stage 2 — layered character motion
- Separate foreground character from scene so Orish can blink, look, wave, point and react while preserving the same approved face, plaits, skin tone and proportions.

## Stage 3 — reusable 3D Orish
- Rigged GLB/glTF character with idle breathing, blink, smile, look, point, wave, celebrate, thinking, discovery reaction, walk, run, jump, crouch and pick-up animations.
- Later lip-sync and game-specific animation.

Continuity is non-negotiable: Orish is six, has plaits/braids, the approved medium-deep brown skin tone, taller age-believable proportions and the approved navy/blue/gold explorer styling.
