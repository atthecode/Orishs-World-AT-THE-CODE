# Orish's World — Level 1 Complete Test Build

Working goal: turn the first test level into a complete, replayable connected experience rather than a single mini-game.

## Level 1 structure
1. Space Signal — station emergency, movement, environmental hazards, timed action, power routing, three-dish triangulation, drone rescue, hidden bay discovery.
2. Cinema Interlude — short in-engine story bridge that reveals the signal has a destination but does not claim alien life.
3. Echo Planet — landing sequence, low-gravity traversal, sample collection, environmental hazards, cave exploration, creature-hypothesis builder and final distant-life tease.
4. Level Complete — combined evidence, badges, stars, replay, progress save and return to world map.

## Gameplay standard
- No major phase can be only a quiz card.
- Player must move, react, investigate, manipulate or decide.
- Mistakes should change the situation or require recovery, not simply say “wrong”.
- Child-safe non-violent pressure only.
- Science language must distinguish evidence from speculation.
- Mobile controls must remain usable on iPhone.

## Character
Use the dedicated `assets/orish-game-walk.webp` sprite for in-game movement. Keep approved HQ art for portraits/comms only.

## Test scope
This Level 1 build is a beta gameplay proof, not production child-data infrastructure. Progress is local-only for the test branch.
