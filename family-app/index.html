Make Lab

Away-from-screen mission

Build a paper aeroplane

  1. Choose one sheet of paper.
  2. Fold it in half, then open it again.
  3. Fold both top corners into the middle line.
  4. Fold again to make a pointed nose.
  5. Fold the wings down.
  6. Test it from a safe open space.

Grown-up: help where needed. Ask the child to measure how far the plane travels using steps or a tape measure.
